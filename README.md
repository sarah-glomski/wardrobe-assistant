# Wardrobe Assistant

A personal outfit planner that helps you actually use your whole closet. Add your clothes with photos, get daily outfit suggestions based on the weather and occasion, and track what you've worn.

## Install on iPhone

1. Open the link in **Safari**
2. Tap the Share button → **Add to Home Screen**
3. Done — it works like an app

## Setup

You need a free Gemini API key for AI photo tagging:

1. Go to [aistudio.google.com](https://aistudio.google.com) → **Get API Key**
2. Open the app → **Settings** → paste your key

Without a key the app still works — you just fill in the tags yourself.

## Features

- **Today** — weather-aware outfit suggestion, pick an occasion, log what you wore
- **Closet** — browse and search your clothes, see what you haven't worn in a while
- **Add item** — take a photo, AI suggests the category/colors/vibe/season
- **History** — calendar of past outfits
- **Settings** — export/import your wardrobe as a backup

## Data & Privacy

Everything is stored on your own device. Photos and wardrobe data never leave your phone except when you use AI tagging (photo is sent to Google's Gemini API using your own key).
